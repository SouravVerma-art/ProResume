# 📖 ProResume AI User Guide

Welcome to ProResume AI! This guide will help you navigate the features of our platform to build a standout resume in minutes.

## 🎯 Getting Started
Once you log in, you can create a new resume or edit an existing one from your dashboard. Click the **"Start Building Free"** button on the homepage to begin.

## 🏗️ The Resume Builder
The builder is divided into three main areas:
1.  **Left Sidebar (Navigation)**: Switch between sections like Personal Info, Experience, and Skills. You can **drag and drop** these sections to change the order they appear on your resume.
2.  **Middle Panel (Editor)**: This is where you enter your data.
3.  **Right Panel (Live Preview)**: Watch your resume update in real-time as you type.

## 🚀 Pro Features

### 1. 🤖 AI Content Assist
In the **Professional Summary** or **Experience** sections, look for the AI Sparkles icon. You can provide a basic description, and our AI will rewrite it to be more impactful and achievement-oriented.

### 2. ⚡ Real-time Autosave
You don't need to click "Save" every time. The system watches your changes and saves them automatically after 2 seconds of inactivity. Look at the bottom footer to see the **"Last Saved"** timestamp.

### 3. 📄 Choosing a Template
Use the **"Template"** dropdown in the header to switch between layouts.
-   **Classic**: Professional and traditional.
-   **Modern**: Contemporary with bold headings and accent colors.
-   **Minimal**: Clean, elegant, and lots of white space.
-   **Regular**: Best for academic or detailed technical CVs.

### 4. 🎨 Accent Colors
Click the **"Accent"** palette icon to change the primary color of your resume. This affects links, icons, and headings depending on your chosen template.

### 5. 🔗 Handling Links
You can add links to your LinkedIn, GitHub, Website, Projects, and Certifications. **Note:** All links will remain clickable in the final PDF download.

## 📥 Exporting & Sharing

### Download PDF
Click the **Download icon** in the top-right corner. The system will generate a high-fidelity PDF.
> **Tip:** If your PDF looks blurry, ensure your browser zoom is set to 100%.

### Live Sharing
If you want to share your resume directly without a file:
1.  Click the **"Private"** button in the header to make it **"Public"**.
2.  Click the **Share icon** to copy your unique resume link.
3.  Anyone with the link can now view your resume online!

---

## 💡 Quick Tips for a Better Resume
- **Be Specific**: Use numbers (e.g., "Increased sales by 20%") instead of vague duties.
- **Check ATS**: Use our **ATS Analysis** tool in the sidebar to see how your resume fares against automated scanners.
- **Keywords**: Ensure the skills mentioned in the job description are also in your **Skills** section.

Happy Job Hunting! 🚀
